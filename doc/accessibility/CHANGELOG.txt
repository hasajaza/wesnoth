WESNOTH ACCESS CHANGELOG

Changes after the first JAWS test (same version number until release)
- Text overlays shown on the map ([print], tutorial hints such as "Move
  Li'sar next to Delfador") are spoken and kept in the F11 history.
- Escape with nothing selected needs a second press for the quit question.
- Home goes to the leader when nothing is selected.
- Popups without a title are no longer called "transient message".
- K / Shift+K list the keeps; Ctrl+R and Alt+R explain when the leader is
  not on a keep, and otherwise point the game at the right castle.
- Tutorial hint titles are separated from the hint text.
- A move is announced before the dialogue it triggers.
- Ctrl+Enter moves the selected unit as close as possible to the cursor;
  hex descriptions name the closest reachable hex for unreachable places;
  the first selection explains how to move.
- Attack dialog: each weapon is read as a sentence ("3 strikes of 6 damage,
  80% chance to hit each"), with the enemy's answer, the chance to kill, the
  chance to die and the expected hit points; the profile buttons say whose
  profile they open; the damage calculation is read as lines (Up/Down),
  including every possible result.
- Attack announcements use the same wording instead of "6-3".
- Ctrl+Enter explains when the unit has no moves left; "1 enemy" instead
  of "1 enemies"; stray spaces before punctuation and the movement-cost
  hexagon symbols in the help are no longer read.
- F7 explains why there is nothing to attack (no selection, already
  attacked, or no enemy within reach, naming the nearest enemy); selecting a
  unit says "no enemy within reach this turn"; Ctrl+Enter onto an enemy
  says "Next to ..." and keeps the unit selected so Enter attacks.
- Short "label: value" pairs are spoken without the colon pause: "HP: 39
  Moves: 6" becomes "hit points 39, moves 6" (HP, XP and MP are spelled
  out); longer sentences keep their colons.
- A dialog that opens while the help is shown (for example the game's quit
  question) now gets the keys instead of the help underneath; returning to
  the game window while the help is open announces the help.
- No stray ": " before list items in change announcements; "1 unit",
  "1 visible enemy", "1 allied unit".
- Lists with column titles (unit list Alt+U, status table Alt+S,
  statistics, and others) read each value with its title: "Type Spearman,
  Name Pike, Moves 5/5, hit points 36/36, level 1, experience 41/42".
- The game's quit question starts on "No"; Tab reaches "Yes".
- Shift+Z: which visible enemies could attack the tile under the cursor
  on their next turn.
- Shift+V opens the unit's details as a list read line by line (Escape
  closes); V gives a short summary with the weapons.
- Campaign list: "Name" and "Timeline" are announced as sort buttons, the
  modifications menu by its name.
- Story pages are spoken once (they were repeated).
- Home and End jump to the first and last item of any list.
- The game map ignores the mouse while accessibility is on (setting
  "Mouse clicks on the game map"): a stray click moved the selected unit to
  the mouse pointer and used up its moves.
- Shift+V lists the unit's traits with their effects (they were missing);
  V names the traits.
- F7 on one of your units (or right after moving it) selects it and lists
  its targets; the game drops the selection after a move.
- NVDA: if Universal Speech does not detect NVDA, the game asks NVDA
  directly (NVDA controller client) and speaks through it; a screen reader
  started after the game is found within a few seconds; the log records the
  NVDA controller's answer, which screen readers were found, and every
  change of speech engine.
- Unnamed fields are named from their id ("txtFilter" is read "filter").
- C also says whether the selected unit can reach the hex this turn, what it
  costs and how many moves would be left, or the total cost and turns needed.
- The help's unit statistics ("HP: 32 Moves: 5") are read without colon
  pauses (the help uses no-break spaces, which were not recognised).
- Permanent download link for the newest tested build (see INSTALL.txt).

0.1.0 (first test version, for Battle for Wesnoth 1.18.8)
- Speech through JAWS, NVDA or SAPI with Universal Speech, on a worker thread.
- All GUI2 menus and dialogs usable with Tab, arrows, Enter, Space, Escape;
  F4 reads the whole dialog.
- Story, character dialogue and objectives read automatically; dialogue
  history with F11.
- Map review cursor with six-direction hex movement, tile and unit
  descriptions, lists of units, enemies, villages, labels, reachable hexes
  and attack options, bookmarks, jump to coordinates.
- Select, move, attack, recruit through the game's own code; strike-by-
  strike combat results; healing, advancement, undo feedback; enemy turn
  announcements limited to what is visible.
- Keyboard reader for the game's help.
- Accessibility settings, command reference, configurable keys, log file.
- Compact package for an existing 1.18.8 installation, with installer and
  uninstaller; nothing in the original installation is changed.
