WESNOTH ACCESS @ACCESS_VERSION@ - KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Verification status
- Tested by a blind player with JAWS and with NVDA on Windows 11: the
  tutorial (both parts) and the start of "A Tale of Two Brothers": menus,
  campaign selection, story, dialogue, moving, attacking, recruiting,
  villages, healing, unit details. Everything else is new to real users;
  reports are welcome.
- Tested automatically on every build (Linux and Windows): main menu,
  settings, command reference, campaign selection, story, dialogue,
  objectives, save and load, map review, unit and village lists,
  hidden-unit protection, select, move, attack, damage calculation,
  advancement, recruit, undo, end of turn, enemy turn, healing, the help,
  unit list, status table, statistics, danger check, mouse protection.
- Not checked one by one: preferences pages, add-ons, multiplayer, hotkey
  settings, map editor dialogs (they use the generic dialog support).

Screens and features with reduced support
- Map editor: its dialogs are readable, but there is no review cursor for
  editing maps.
- Online multiplayer: lobby and game setup dialogs are readable through the
  generic layer; chat messages are spoken. Not tested.
- The "Damage Calculation" graph is read as a list of possible hit points
  with their probabilities.
- The minimap, unit portraits and story images are not described.
- The whiteboard (planning mode) has no special support.
- "Show enemy moves" (the game's highlight of where enemies can go) is not
  shown as a map; Shift+Z tells which visible enemies can attack the tile
  under the cursor next turn.
- Turn timers of timed multiplayer games are not announced.
- Accessibility messages are in English only (the game's own texts follow
  the selected language).

Behaviour to be aware of
- In the map view the letters W, E, D, S, A and Q move the review cursor.
  The game's own D (unit help) and S (statistics) are on Ctrl+Alt+D and
  Ctrl+Alt+S; Ctrl+G (grid) is "jump to coordinates".
- Right after an attack or advancement animation, game commands are blocked
  for a moment by the game; you then hear "not available now".
- In story screens, the first Enter on a page that is still appearing only
  shows it completely; the next Enter goes to the next page.
- Recruiting places the unit on the hex under the review cursor if it is a
  free castle hex, otherwise on a castle hex chosen by the game.
- Character dialogue and story texts are spoken when they appear and stay
  in the dialogue history (F11). Very long texts can be read sentence by
  sentence with Tab and Up/Down.
- Typed characters in text fields are echoed by the screen reader itself
  (its "speak typed characters" setting).

Packaging
- The overlay package needs an existing Battle for Wesnoth 1.18.8
  installation; other versions are refused or warned about.
- Only test builds are published (the "access-latest" release, replaced by
  every build that passes the tests); there are no numbered releases yet.
